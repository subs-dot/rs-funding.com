Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

RS Funding ("RS Funding," "we," "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes what we collect when you use our website or apply for funding, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Application details: business name, industry, state, time in business, deposit ranges, requested amount, and use of funds.
  • Contact details: your name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Underwriting materials: bank statements, ID, and other documents you provide later in the process.
  • Third-party data: credit reports, public records, and identity verification results obtained to evaluate your application.
  • Usage data: browser, device, pages viewed, and referral source, collected through cookies and similar tools.

Why we use it

To evaluate your application, prepare and present offers, communicate with you, verify your identity, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and improve our services.

Who we share it with

Funding partners and service providers that help us review applications, fund advances, and run our business; credit bureaus and verification services; and government authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS and phone communications

If you opt in, we may call or text you about funding offers. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies

We use cookies for site functionality, analytics, and advertising measurement. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Security and retention

We protect your information with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your state, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us below to make a request.

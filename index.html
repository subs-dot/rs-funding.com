// 03 — Repayment

Payments that flex with your sales.

With a merchant cash advance, you sell a portion of your future receivables at an agreed price. Remittances come out of your business account automatically, and you can choose the structure that fits how your money comes in.

Percentage of sales

A set percentage of your daily card sales or deposits. On a slow day you remit less, and on a busy day you remit more and finish sooner.

Fixed daily or weekly ACH

A predictable, fixed amount based on your average revenue, debited each business day or week. Easy to plan around. If revenue drops, you can request a reconciliation.

// 05 — FAQ

Straight answers.

Still have questions? Email admin@rs-funding.com and a real person will get back to you.

What is a merchant cash advance, in plain English?

We buy a slice of your business's future sales at a discount and give you the cash today. You pay it back automatically as your sales come in, until the agreed amount has been delivered. It's not a loan, so there's no interest rate.

How is the cost calculated?

With a factor rate, usually written as a decimal like 1.25. Multiply your advance by the factor rate to get the total payback. For example, a $50,000 advance at 1.25 means $62,500 in total. It doesn't compound. Any fees are spelled out in your offer, and depending on your state you may also receive additional cost disclosures.

Will a slow month hurt me?

With percentage-of-sales remittance, your payments shrink when your sales do. With fixed ACH, you can request a reconciliation so your remittances reflect what you actually took in.

Can I pay it off early?

Some agreements include an early-payoff discount. Ask your advisor before you sign, and we'll show you exactly how early payoff works on your offer.

What do I need to apply?

A quick email with a few details about your business, then your last three months of business bank statements, a driver's license, and a voided business check. That's usually everything.

Does applying cost anything or lock me in?

No. Applying is free, and you're under no obligation to accept an offer. If the numbers don't work for you, walk away.

I already have an advance. Can I still apply?

Possibly. We'll review your existing obligations and only make an offer if your cash flow can comfortably support it. Taking on more than your business can handle isn't good for either of us.
